Privacy Policy: Clara's Wildlife Adventure

JobRhino LLC · Effective November 19, 2026

Clara's Wildlife Adventure is a game for young children. It is made to collect nothing. This page explains exactly what the app keeps and where.

The short version

  • We collect no personal information, from children or from adults.
  • The app has no internet access. It asks Android for no permissions at all, so it cannot go online, use the camera or microphone, read contacts, or find your location.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no purchases inside the app, and no links that leave the app.

What stays on your device

To remember each child's progress, the app saves a small file on the device itself:

  • the nickname and picture a grown-up chooses for each child (a nickname can be anything, it does not need to be a real name);
  • which animals the child has rescued, and the difficulty level for each skill;
  • grown-up settings such as narration on or off.

This file never leaves the device. JobRhino LLC cannot see it, and nobody else receives it. If Android's own device backup is turned on, the system may include app data in your personal backup under your Google account, as it does for most apps; that backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Deleting the data

In the grown-ups area (behind a question for adults), you can remove a child's profile, which deletes its progress from the device. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved.

Children

The game is designed for children aged about 2 to 8. Because it collects no personal information, it is built to respect the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and Google Play's Families policies. The grown-ups area is protected by a parent gate.

Changes

If a future version ever changed what the app keeps, we would update this page and the date above before releasing it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to JobRhino LLC at the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
